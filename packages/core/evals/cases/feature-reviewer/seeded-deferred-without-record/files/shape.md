# Shape: Clear read books

- AC-1: WHEN the person clears read books from "Dune", "Emma" (read) and "Ubik", THE list SHALL show "Dune" then "Ubik".
- AC-2: WHEN the person clears read books, THE app SHALL show how many were cleared with an Undo button for 10 seconds.
- AC-3: WHEN the person chooses Undo within 10 seconds, THE cleared books SHALL come back in their places.
