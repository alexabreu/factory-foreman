# Shape: Clear read books

Problem: finished books crowd out the ones people still want to read.
People: readers with 20 or more books.

Criteria:

- AC-1: WHEN the person clears read books, THE list SHALL show only unread books.
- AC-2: WHEN the person clears read books, THE app SHALL offer the cleared books as a CSV download.
- AC-3: WHEN the person chooses "Clear read", THE app SHALL ask "Clear 3 read books?" before clearing.

Out of scope: syncing across devices.

Open questions: should clearing ask for confirmation first, or clear at once and offer undo? Not decided yet.
